Before your first card

A quick look at Grant Control.

Lead a fictional control-engineering lab as its principal investigator (PI). Each round, take two actions: solve a Research question, fund a Validation test, or mentor a new PhD researcher.

Full game · 3–5 players · 6 rounds

Two Results and one Validation in the same topic make a Paper. Papers and topic fit help you win grants; Funding supports PhD researchers and further validation. After six rounds, the strongest research program—papers, active researchers, and new topics—wins.
