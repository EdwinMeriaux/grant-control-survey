The game in one loop

Full game · 3–5 players · 6 rounds

Each round, you have two actions: answer a Research card, attempt a funded Validation card, or use a Mentorship card to recruit a PhD researcher.

  1. 1

    Do research

    Answer a control question correctly to earn a Result.

  2. 2

    Check the evidence

    Spend 1 Funding and answer a Validation card correctly to earn a Validation.

  3. 3

    Publish a paper

    Combine 2 Results + 1 Validation in the same topic to make a Paper.

  4. 4

    Win a grant

    Compete for Funding using your publication record and fit with the grant’s topic.

  5. 5

    Support your lab

    Use Funding to keep PhD researchers active and pay for more validation.

↻Funding keeps the loop going. Each active PhD researcher gets an extra research attempt per round. A larger lab can produce more evidence, but each researcher needs 1 Funding to stay active next round.

How you win

After six rounds, score 2 points per Paper, 1 per active PhD researcher, and 1 per new lab topic. The highest score wins.
